# Licensed sports checklist expansion

User-approved scope, October 28, 2026: all historical officially licensed sports releases indexed by PriceCharting / SportsCardsPro. This supersedes the previous 2019-onward import target and Topps-only expansion priority. It does not declare every provider record licensed or publish unreviewed records automatically.

## Coverage ledger

Maintain one row per exact release, including sport, printed year, publisher/brand, provider console ID, official licensing evidence, checklist source and hash, website release ID, imported sections, missing sections, publication status, and separate photo/link/price coverage.

Provider directories establish what the provider indexes. They are not licensing evidence. Review the release's actual league/team rights and period; a player-association license alone is not a league/team license. Do not exclude an entire publisher merely because some of its other releases are unlicensed. Paper, Chrome, Update, Sapphire, regional editions, inserts and autograph subsets must retain distinct identities.

Use explicit stages: provider indexed, licensing verified, checklist reviewed, published, and references verified. Unknown licensing stays in review. Historical checklists may require a preserved publisher checklist or the provider's actual numbered checklist paired with independent primary licensing evidence; do not infer missing card numbers or teams.

## Newest-first completion order (user approved October 29, 2026)

Prioritize released sets by verified release date, newest first, across the approved licensed sports scope. A printed year alone does not establish release order; verify dates before choosing the first release. Do not start, replace or reorder the collector queue through this catalog priority change.

Concentrate on one exact release at a time. Reconcile its full official master checklist: base, inserts, autograph and relic subsets, physical variations and release-specific parallel eligibility. Then verify every published printing against exact provider records, image health and holds, source links and available prices. Verify live discovery, pagination, card pages and selectors against the resulting inventory.

Track checklist, photos, source links and prices separately with exact denominators and itemized unresolved identities. Sample checks cannot establish full-set completeness. A missing provider photo or price is an external coverage gap, not permission to invent or substitute data. Do not label the release 100% complete while any required dimension remains unverified or missing. Report an actionable blocker explicitly rather than silently moving on or endlessly rescanning unchanged records.

Price refresh should be on-demand for viewed, exactly matched product IDs, with a shared stale cache and account-wide provider rate limiting; it must not scan the full catalog. This newly requested feature is separate from the previously deferred recurring whole-source photo/price refresh.
