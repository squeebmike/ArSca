Three checks before buying

  1. Measure the comic’s width and height, including cover overhang.
  2. Choose a bag wide enough for both the book and the board. Annuals, square-bound books, and thicker issues may need a wider bag.
  3. Check the exact BCW SKU, pack quantity, and product dimensions. Bag dimensions below exclude the closure flap.

Never force a comic into a tight bag. Keep adhesive flaps away from the cover, and use clean hands and a flat work surface.

Modern & Current comics

BCW’s Modern board is 6⅝ × 10½ inches (1-BBMOD). Its Current board is slightly wider at 6¾ × 10½ inches (1-BBCUR). Standard Modern/Current bags are 6⅞ × 10½ inches (1-CUR); thick-comic versions are wider.

Choose Current boards for extra width around a typical current comic, while checking your book’s actual fit.

Silver Age & Regular comics

Silver-size supplies suit many wider older comics, but publication age alone does not guarantee a fit. BCW’s Regular board measures 6⅞ × 10½ inches (1-BBREG); the Silver board is 7 × 10½ inches (1-BBSIL).

The standard Silver/Regular bag is 7⅛ × 10½ inches (1-SIL). Silver thick-comic bags are 7¼ inches wide. For a simple starting pair, compare the standard Silver bag with the Silver backing board below.

Golden Age comics

BCW’s Golden board measures 7½ × 10½ inches (1-BBGOL), and the standard Golden bag measures 7⅝ × 10½ inches (1-GOL). Golden thick-comic bags are wider at 8 inches.

Older comics vary in size and condition. Measure carefully and leave comfortable clearance for fragile edges.

Polypropylene, Mylar, or premade bags and boards?

  • Polypropylene bags: a practical everyday option, including resealable and wider thick-comic versions.
  • Mylar bags: BCW offers 2 MIL and 4 MIL options. Their dimensions and pack quantities differ; check the matching SKU.
  • Premade bags and boards: boards are already inserted in the bags. Check the number of complete sets per pack.

Keep comics away from sunlight, moisture, heat, and excessive pressure. A bag and board cannot correct poor storage conditions.
