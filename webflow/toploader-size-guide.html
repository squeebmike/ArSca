BCW toploader & card thickness size guide

Choose protection by measuring your card’s width, height, and thickness. A card’s set name alone cannot tell you which holder fits.

Start with the printable thickness gauge

  1. Download the BCW gauge PDF and print at 100% / Actual size. Turn off “Fit to page.”
  2. Check the print scale with a ruler, then compare the edge of your card to the gauge.
  3. Read the correct column: BCW toploaders and magnetic holders use different size steps.
  4. For a toploader, use a suitable soft sleeve and allow clearance. Never force a card into a holder or press a magnetic lid closed against it.

Do not measure against the image on your screen. Screen size, browser zoom, and image scaling change its physical size. 1 PT = 0.001 inch; 1,000 PT = 1 inch (25.4 mm).

Toploader sizes and magnetic holder sizes

ProtectionSizes shown on BCW’s gaugeHow to choose
Toploader20, 59, 79, 108, 138, 168, 197, 240, 360 PTMatch the measured card and allow room for the sleeve. Check the product’s actual dimensions.
Magnetic holder35, 55, 75, 100, 130, 180, 360 PTMatch the recessed card cavity to the card. Follow the holder’s sleeve and fit instructions.

Thickness is only one measurement. Standard trading cards, tobacco cards, tall cards, and booklets may need different width and height formats.

Which BCW holder fits a 130 PT card?

For a standard-format card around 130 PT thick, compare BCW’s 138 PT thick-card toploader (SKU 1-TLCH-TH-3.5MM) with its 130 PT magnetic card holder (SKU 1-MCH-130). These are different holder styles, not interchangeable size labels.

The 138 PT toploader holds cards up to its stated thickness; check the sleeved card’s fit. The 130 PT magnetic holder requires a card that fits its cavity without pressure. If the lid does not close freely, stop and remeasure.

Sleeve first, then protect

Use an appropriately sized soft sleeve before inserting a card into a toploader. Thicker cards may need thick-card sleeves. Keep the opening free of grit, handle the card by its edges, and insert it gently. Do not assume every magnetic holder is designed to take a sleeved card.

Board game cards: measure width and height

This BCW guide matches board game cards to sleeves by millimeter dimensions. It measures the card’s footprint, not its thickness. Measure the card itself and check the sleeve product’s fit description.
